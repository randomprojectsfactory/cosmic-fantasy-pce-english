# Credits and localisation sources

## Original game

**Laser Soft / Telenet Japan** — Cosmic Fantasy: Bouken Shounen Yuu. The original writing, characters, artwork, music, Japanese performances and production credits belong to their original creators. The original in-game staff credits are retained.

## Existing English localisation

The **Cosmic Fantasy Collection localisation for Switch** is the basis for the English script and terminology. Credit for that existing English localisation remains with its original localisation team and rightsholders. This project does not claim authorship of the original English translation.

## This PCE adaptation

Work specific to this adaptation includes the native English text implementation, UI and shop layout, wrapping and pagination, cinematic subtitle renderer and timing, bilingual location caption, display corrections, and correction of actor/target ordering in combat messages.

## Tools

[xdelta3](https://github.com/jmacd/xdelta), by Joshua MacDonald and contributors, provides the binary patch format. It is required separately and is not bundled. No emulator or BIOS is included.

Naming a source does not imply its creators' involvement, approval or endorsement.
